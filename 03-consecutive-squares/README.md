# Primes of the form k² + (k+1)²

## Status

- finite identities and prime counts: **exact finite checks**;
- singular series value printed by the script: **truncated numerical product** over primes below
  `10^7`;
- prime-count prediction and the 4:2:2:1 asymptotic residue split: **Bateman–Horn
  conjectural/conditional interpretation**.

No novelty claim is made.

## Object

For `f(k)=2k²+2k+1`,

`f(k)=k²+(k+1)²=((2k+1)²+1)/2`.

The question whether infinitely many values are prime is open; this computation does not bear
on that open problem.

## Singular series

For odd prime `p`, `f(k)=0 (mod p)` is equivalent to `(2k+1)²=-1 (mod p)`. Thus the number
of roots is two for `p=1 (mod 4)` and zero for `p=3 (mod 4)`; at `p=2`, `f(k)` is always odd.
The script evaluates the corresponding product truncated at `p<10^7`:

`2.7455780570...`

The constant is derived from these local root counts, not fitted to the observed prime counts.

At `k<=150000`, the four relevant reduced residue classes mod 30 occur with counts
`8136, 4110, 4084, 2074` for classes `1, 11, 13, 23` respectively. The transfer of the CRT
weights `4:2:2:1` to prime values requires Bateman–Horn applied to the corresponding
sub-progressions; it does not follow from a bare positive-density assertion.
