# Provenance and attribution

This file records the provenance policy for the public release. It is not a claim of authorship over
classical mathematics.

## Provenance classes

### Classical / external

Results, mechanisms and terminology that predate this project are attributed to the literature.
Examples in this release include Bateman–Horn, Rubinstein–Sarnak, finite character theory,
Parseval/orthogonality and elementary congruence facts.

### Project-specific computation

Scripts, finite data products, exact counts, regression snapshots and release engineering produced
for this repository are project artefacts. Their authorship does not imply novelty of the mathematics
they instantiate.

### Historical family material

Where an idea can be traced to Bernard Couret, that attribution is made only when a primary source
supports it. Similarity alone is not treated as authorship or priority.

### Contemporary reconstruction

Arguments, reorganisations or implementations produced during the 2026 Couret–Unification
program are labelled as contemporary reconstruction when they systematise older material or
standard mathematics.

### AI-assisted work

AI systems were used during parts of the broader research workflow for drafting, checking,
red-teaming, code assistance and literature orientation. They are not treated as authors. Public
claims remain the responsibility of the human author and are governed by the repository's
status files, code, CI and cited sources.

## Priority rule

For every public statement, the priority order is:

1. exact source artefact or executable result;
2. current status/claim-boundary document;
3. dated correction or changelog;
4. synthesis or retrospective report.

A later summary never silently overrides a primary artefact.

## Negative results and demotions

The research program intentionally keeps a record of claims that were restricted, demoted,
identified as classical, or refuted. Those events are part of the provenance of the program and
must not be erased to create a cleaner-looking historical narrative.

## Release boundary

This file documents provenance for the material actually released here. It does not authorize
publication of private research notes, personal correspondence, FCI material, or other projects
that were deliberately excluded from this repository.
