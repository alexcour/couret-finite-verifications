## Research implication

A successful Couret–OpenAI bridge should therefore **not** be sought as a fixed periodic weight whose role is merely to combine the eight mod-30 channels.

The next viable classes are:

1. **scale-dependent weights** (w_X);
2. **conductor-dependent compensation** (w_q);
3. a justified **noninvertible projection** with a theorem explaining why the discarded modes are harmless;
4. an exact second representation that changes scale, analogous in function (not necessarily form) to Poisson/reflection;
5. passage to richer character systems, beginning with the order-6 structure visible at (U(210)), while keeping clear that this is not yet the Hecke setting of OpenAI.
