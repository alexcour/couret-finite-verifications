# NTK-OBS-05 — Selected pre-existing literature (not exhaustive)

1. Dmitry Batenkov and Yosef Yomdin, **On the Accuracy of Solving Confluent Prony Systems**, *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics* 73(1), 134–154 (2013). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1137/110836584 ; open preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1137 . Conditioning and optimal-recovery perspective for Prony-type systems.
2. Dmitry Batenkov, **Decimated generalized Prony systems** (2013), https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.0753 . Arithmetic-progression sampling and robustness.
3. Dmitry Batenkov and Yosef Yomdin, **Local and global geometry of Prony systems and Fourier reconstruction of piecewise-smooth functions** (2013 preprint), https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.1187 . Geometry and stability of inverse moment models.
4. Rami Katz, Nuha Diab, and Dmitry Batenkov, **On the accuracy of Prony's method for recovery of exponential sums with closely spaced exponents**, *Applied and Computational Harmonic Analysis* 73 (2024), article 101687. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acha.2024.101687 . Minimax stability in the distinct super-resolution regime studied there; not imported as a proof for our current different noise model.
5. Classical deterministic minimax/optimal-recovery two-point indistinguishability, monotone inversion and elementary mean-value/Rolle inequalities: these are general mathematical background, **not** newly discovered here.
6. Internal NTK-OBS predecessors: `research/NTK_OBS_01/ROBUSTNESS_01`, `SAMPLING_02`, `ADAPTIVE_03`, `CONTINUOUS_04` in `alexcour/couret-finite-verifications`, branch `research/ntk-obs-01-2026-10-11` (11 October 2026).

This list is not a complete prior-art search. Mathematical claims in this package are confined to explicit elementary special cases and carry no novelty or priority assertion.
