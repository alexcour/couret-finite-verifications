# Prior art and mathematical prerequisites (not a priority claim)

1. Daniel Potts and Manfred Tasche, *Parameter estimation for nonincreasing exponential sums by Prony-like methods*, Linear Algebra and its Applications 439 (2013), 1024-1039. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2012.10.036 . Classical discrete exponential recovery.
2. Dmitry Batenkov and Yosef Yomdin, *On the Accuracy of Solving Confluent Prony Systems*, SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics 73 (2013), 134-154. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1137/110836584 . Conditioning and near-colliding modes.
3. Dmitry Batenkov, *Decimated generalized Prony systems*, arXiv:1308.0753 (2013), https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.0753 . Sampling in arithmetic progressions and noise robustness.
4. Samuel Karlin and William J. Studden, *Tchebycheff Systems: With Applications in Analysis and Statistics*, Interscience (1966). Bibliographic record: https://books.google.com/books?id=P7Y-AAAAIAAJ . Classical extended Chebyshev systems and root-counting.
5. Standard implicit function theorem and elementary Rolle's theorem, used as general-purpose pre-existing tools. No priority claim.
6. Internal predecessor: NTK-OBS-03 (2026-10-11), archived at research/NTK_OBS_01/ADAPTIVE_03 on github.com/alexcour/couret-finite-verifications (branch research/ntk-obs-01-2026-10-11). Its four centers are reused; this continuation was not preregistered independently.

This list is selective and not an exhaustive literature review of active sensing, optimal experiment design, finite rate of innovation, or spectral estimation.
