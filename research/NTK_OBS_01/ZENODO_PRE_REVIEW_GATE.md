# NTK-OBS-01 — Zenodo pre-review decision (11 October 2026)

**Decision:** An **UNREVIEWED RESEARCH ARCHIVE / NOT PEER REVIEWED** may be deposited on Zenodo **before** human external review, once the exact, frozen candidate passes the separate archival gate below. This decision supersedes previous absolute prohibitions on a DOI motivated *solely* by the lack of external review; it does not certify science, originality, legal rights or archival readiness. The PR stays in **draft**. No merge, stable release, HAL or arXiv submission is authorized by this note.

**Archival gate, currently HOLD pending final checks:** (1) self-contained scientific files, scripts, results, deterministic replay and environment; (2) passed integrity manifest and explicit CI/local test status of the frozen version; (3) E/N/Q/D status, finite scope, negative findings, counterexamples and prior art described without novelty/priority inflation; (4) rights and licences checked separately for code/text/data; (5) privacy/confidentiality/patent checks, especially private Google Docs links; (6) version, Git commit, date, SHA-256, ORCID, AI assistance, unreviewed disclaimer and metadata checked. Only when all pass can the creator upload and register the DOI actually issued.

External scientific review remains OPEN and is actively sought after public pre-review archiving. Preserve original versions and issue errata/new archival versions for future corrections. This file changes dissemination governance only, not mathematical claims or source history.
