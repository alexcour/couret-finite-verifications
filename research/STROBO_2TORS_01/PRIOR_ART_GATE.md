# Prior-art boundary — pending theorem-level comparison

This algebraic classification is an **internal research candidate**, not a novelty or priority assertion. Relevant literature distinguishes ordered representations (all pairs, including diagonal), distinct summands and unordered representations, sumsets and subset-sum multisets; these are not interchangeable.

- Quan-Hui Yang, Feng-Juan Chen, *Partitions of Z_m with the same representation functions* (2012), https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/53/ajc_v53_p257.pdf . Specific complement/partition assumptions; possible overlap with cosets of order-six construction.
- Shi-Qiang Chen, Xiao-Hui Yan, *Partitions of Z_m with identical representation function* (2021; published online 2020), https://doi.org/10.1017/S0004972720000945 . Ordered representation; compare statement assumptions rather than merely matching terminology.
- Cui-Fang Sun, Meng-Chi Xiong, *On a problem of partitions of Z_m with the same representation functions* (2021), https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.16513 . Ordered invariant with union/intersection restrictions described by prior STROBO audit.
- Keleti & Kolountzakis (2006), triple correlations/decks, https://doi.org/10.61091/ojac-104 . Distinct invariant, not direct theorem equivalence.
- Ciprietti & Glaudo (2025), subset sums of all subsets, https://doi.org/10.5070/C65365553 . Distinct invariant.
