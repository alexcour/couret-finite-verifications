## Promotion rule

A public claim may be promoted only from its own contract coordinates. In particular:

- `Q: FORMALIZED` does not imply `N: ORIGINAL`.
- `E: EXACT-FINITE` does not imply a broader `S`.
- `D: PUBLIC` does not imply review.
- successful replay does not imply novelty.
- failure of replay lowers Q until explained; it does not automatically refute the mathematical statement.
