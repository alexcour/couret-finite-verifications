# Prior-art gate and terminology — 11 October 2026

**No novelty or priority claim. No external scientific endorsement.**

Relevant primary-source literature on the ordered representation function:

- S.-Q. Chen and X.-H. Yan (2021), *Partitions of Z_m with identical representation function*, *Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society* 103(2), 204–209, https://doi.org/10.1017/S0004972720000945 . The publisher explicitly defines the invariant as an **ordered** sum count, exactly the kind used for `A_S^2`. Theorem 1.2 includes the appropriate order-six partition example; it does not, on the paper's stated hypotheses alone, imply an exhaustive classification of all cardinality-three pairs at all orders.
- Q.-H. Yang and F.-J. Chen (2012), *Partitions of Z_m with the same representation functions*, *Australasian Journal of Combinatorics* 53, 257–262, https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/53/ajc_v53_p257.pdf . Relevant representation-function classification with specific structural hypotheses.
- Q.-H. Yang and M. Tang (2017), *Representation functions on finite sets with extreme symmetric differences*, *Journal of Number Theory* 180, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2017.03.024 . The abstract distinguishes ordered, distinct-unordered and unordered representations; compare the **correct** definition before invoking a theorem.
- N. H. Bshouty (2018), *Exact learning from an honest teacher that answers membership queries*, *Theoretical Computer Science* 733, 4–43, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2018.04.034 . General prior work on finite concept class identification and query complexity.
- N. H. Bshouty and C. A. Haddad-Zaknoon (2019), *Adaptive Exact Learning of Decision Trees from Membership Queries*, *Proceedings of Machine Learning Research* 98, 207–234, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v98/bshouty19a.html . Different problem class; confirms that adaptive/fixed query complexity is a **classical** theme.

**Warning:** An article named similarly to the Cambridge ordered-representation paper can use unordered pair sums; a title match is not a theorem-level equivalence. Full-text side-by-side audit of later general representation-function and exact-learning results remains an open gate.
