# Ordered representation functions: prior art (11 October 2026)

Chen and Yan, *Partitions of Z_m with identical representation function*, Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society 103 (2021), 204-209, DOI 10.1017/S0004972720000945, study the same **ordered** sum-representation function as our adjacency-square invariant. Their published result covers the relevant partition configuration in Z/6Z. The example {0,1,4} versus {0,2,5} therefore has a published antecedent. Its embeddings inside cyclic subgroups of order six must not be described as a novel construction.

The paper's partition hypotheses do **not** automatically yield an exhaustive classification of every cardinality-three support in every cyclic group. That exhaustive claim and the proposed probe-minimality claims still require an independent proof audit and theorem-level literature comparison. No priority or novelty is claimed. Keep the research PR in draft.
