# Prior art and referee review boundary (2026-10-11)

This is a LIMITED targeted bibliography, not an exhaustive priority search.

- Q.-H. Yang and F.-J. Chen, "Partitions of Z_m with the same representation functions", *Australasian Journal of Combinatorics* **53** (2012), 257-262. https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/53/ajc_v53_p257.pdf . This primary source distinguishes ordered pair counts (sigma_1) from two unordered variants. Theorem 2 classifies equality between a set and its COMPLEMENT, not all arbitrary pairs; Problem 2 explicitly requests arbitrary-pair classification. Hence its displayed theorem alone does not exhaust square-equal supports of size three, although the paper is important prior art.

- C.-F. Sun and M.-C. Xiong, "On a problem of partitions of Z_m with the same representation functions", *Periodica Mathematica Hungarica* (2021), doi:10.1007/s10998-021-00423-9, https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.16513 . The abstract describes ORDERED pair counts, but its classification assumes A union B = Z_m and intersection sizes 2 or m-2, not arbitrary sparse triple supports. Its full theorems still need direct inspection before novelty claims.

- S.-Q. Chen and X.-H. Yan, "Partitions of Z_m with identical representation function", *Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society* **103** (2021), 204-209, doi:10.1017/S0004972720000945. https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bulletin-of-the-australian-mathematical-society/article/partitions-of-mathbb-zm-with-identical-representation-function/6A10D33333A7B22D71C5AF1EA783F2C1 . Its ordered-invariant theorem assumes symmetric-difference cardinality m-2, a different general constraint; precise overlap in small n has NOT been completely checked.

- N. H. Bshouty and C. A. Haddad-Zaknoon, "Adaptive Exact Learning of Decision Trees from Membership Queries", ALT/PMLR **98** (2019), 207-234. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v98/bshouty19a.html . Important general adaptive/nonadaptive query-complexity precedent; no assertion of identical cost model.

Q (finite checks): locally reproduced; independent implementation, not an external expert.
N (novelty): UNRESOLVED; verify representation-function conventions and hypotheses before claims.
