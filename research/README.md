# Research working area

Status clarified on 20 October 2026.

This directory is a working research and review area on the current branch. It is outside the preserved public v1.0.0 release and its version DOI. A link to that DOI does not archive or validate these later files.

Each item must identify its exact statement, assumptions, source commit, executable evidence where available, and separate mathematical, human-review and prior-art statuses. A finite replay certifies only its stated finite scope. A successful Lean build must be tied to its source and printed axioms. Neither establishes originality.

Existing subdirectory notices and open-review questions retain their individual scope. Historical corrections and failed approaches are preserved. No blanket certification is assigned to this directory.

## Remediation priorities — 20 October 2026

STROBO and the separate Cayley support-translation family are the two priorities for completing exact statement/evidence mapping, primary-source comparison and external review. Other research strands are on hold for promotion and new dissemination. Their existing archives, diagnostics and corrections remain available; work that closes an identified debt or repairs a counterexample is permitted.

Before resuming another strand, record its exact exportable statement, executable or written witness, prior-art comparison, review limits and a human decision to reopen it. No new stable research release or archival deposit is justified by CI alone while the relevant prior-art gate remains open. After an audit failure, resolve the corresponding debt or record a verified blocker before producing further research output on that strand.

This is a work-priority and publication rule. It does not delete Git branches or certify that every repository is technically protected by CI. The RH repository has its own machine-enforced status manifest; extending that enforcement to all public surfaces remains a separate task.
