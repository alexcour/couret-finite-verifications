# Research working area

Status clarified on 20 October 2026.

This directory is a working research and review area on the current branch. It is outside the preserved public v1.0.0 release and its version DOI. A link to that DOI does not archive or validate these later files.

Each item must identify its exact statement, assumptions, source commit, executable evidence where available, and separate mathematical, human-review and prior-art statuses. A finite replay certifies only its stated finite scope. A successful Lean build must be tied to its source and printed axioms. Neither establishes originality.

Existing subdirectory notices and open-review questions retain their individual scope. Historical corrections and failed approaches are preserved. No blanket certification is assigned to this directory.
