# AI assistance disclosure

AI systems were used during parts of the wider research workflow for drafting, code assistance, literature orientation, adversarial checking, and editorial review.

They are **not** listed as authors.

The public claims in this repository are supported by the cited literature, deterministic scripts, finite outputs, and the local status labels attached to each computation. Responsibility for release decisions, claim boundaries, and attribution remains with Alexandre Couret.

AI output is not treated as evidence of mathematical truth or historical priority. AI-assisted suggestions were required to survive executable checks or source review before inclusion in the public package.

This disclosure documents the role of AI assistance without publishing private conversations or unrelated unreleased material.
